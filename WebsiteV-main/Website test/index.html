Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you start.

Is V+ safe to use?

V+ is built with safety in mind and bypasses are updated regularly. That said, using any client on a server carries risk — use it at your own discretion and preferably on an alt account.

What servers does V+ support?

V+ is optimized for DonutSMP and other anarchy / SMP servers running Minecraft 1.21 and newer. Many modules also work on practice and PvP servers.

How do I get started after purchase?

You'll get instant access to your dashboard after checkout. Download the V+ launcher, sign in with your key, pick your Minecraft version and hit launch. Setup takes about a minute.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If V+ isn't for you, contact us within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund. See the refund policy for the details.

How often is V+ updated?

We ship updates weekly, and whenever Minecraft or a major server changes something that affects the client. Your launcher keeps itself up to date automatically.

Can I use V+ on multiple devices?

Your license is tied to one HWID (computer). Need to switch? Request a free HWID reset in our Discord and our team will sort it out.

What about anti-cheat detection?

Our bypasses are maintained continuously and our configs use human-like randomization. No client can promise zero risk, so we recommend subtle settings on accounts you care about.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11, Java 21, Minecraft Java Edition 1.21+ with Fabric, and at least 4 GB of RAM allocated to the game. A dedicated GPU is recommended for the visual modules.

Is there customer support if I run into issues?

Yes — our Discord support is open 24/7. Open a ticket and a team member will help you with installation, configs or licensing.

How is V+ different from other clients?

V+ combines combat, utility bots and visuals in one launcher with no add-ons or upsells. Every plan includes all 14 bots, all modules and every future update.
